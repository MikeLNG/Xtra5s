Xtra5s - feature list (version 1.73.3)
Everything official Xtra does, plus the features below.

========================================
Always up to date
- Stays current with official Xtra: new versions arrive automatically and install with one tap
  from inside the app.
- Installs alongside official Xtra and Xtra5, so you can keep all of them.
- See what's new in official Xtra right from Settings.
- Settings > Shortcuts lists every extra press and gesture, grouped by screen.

========================================
Dual Stream
- Watch two live streams at once from the Dual Stream tab, side by side on one screen (stacked
  in portrait).
  - Tap + and type a channel for Stream 1 and Stream 2 (suggestions from the channels you
    follow), each with its own quality (720p by default) and its own mute and volume.
  - Show both chats with the chat button (hidden when Dual Stream opens), with your
    highlights. Chat doesn't work properly in Dual Stream.
  - Tap a video to pause it (playing again jumps to live), mute it or set its
    volume (the number matches how loud it sounds), change its quality, reload it, or make it
    fullscreen.
  - Touch, hold and slide left / right on a video to set its volume, with a volume bar.
  - Swap the two streams' places with one button.
  - Put another channel in one stream's place (Replace, or swipe down on it), or keep just one
    stream in the normal player (Watch only this, or swipe up on it).
  - Offline channels start playing by themselves when they go live; dropped streams
    reconnect on their own.
  - Battery percentage in the corner, fading out of the way like in the player.
  - Recent pairs, with both profile pictures and a LIVE badge on whoever is live, are one
    tap away; name suggestions show profile pictures too.

========================================
Chat
- Highlight the people you care about: give any chatter's messages a colored background.
  - Organize them into named groups (for example Mods or Friends), each with its own color.
  - Change a color, rename, move or remove anyone in a couple of taps, and see the result live
    in the chat while you choose.
  - Open your highlights straight from the video player; in landscape the panels sit beside
    the chat and use the extra width. A second tab there sets the special message colors too.
  - Pick a default group, then long-press anyone in chat to add them to it (or take them out).
- A full color picker: color wheel, hex codes, brightness and transparency, presets and your
  own saved colors, with a realistic chat preview.
- Color-code special messages: subs and announcements, first-time chatters, channel point
  rewards, mentions of you, bot messages, and cheers (bits), with a minimum number of bits so
  you can pick out the bigger ones (like TTS cheers).
  - Common bots are recognized out of the box, and you can add your own.
- Make chat your own: message spacing, separator lines, font choice and username size.
- Animated emotes play in sync across the whole chat.
- Tap an emote to see its details; tap a message to see the conversation.
- Long-press an emote in chat to put it in your message, ready to send.
- Emote suggestions as you type, no ":" needed.
- Conversation and emote popups open beside the chat, so the video stays visible.
- Compact reply lines that show more of the original message.

========================================
Watching
- Built for TV remotes (also keyboards and game controllers):
  - Left / Right to skip back or forward by your chosen amount, with the seek bar showing.
  - Up to scrub through the video, by tapping or holding Left / Right, then OK to jump and
    fine-tune with Left / Right.
  - Down for chapters (or to show / hide chat on live streams), hold OK to reload a stream,
    Back to close a minimized video.
  - Clear outlines on the selected card, and channel / game banners that move out of the way
    as you browse down.
- Touch, hold and slide left / right on the video to set the volume, with a volume bar; the
  volume number matches how loud it sounds.
- On VODs, touch and hold the bottom of the video and drag to rewind or fast-forward: each
  step is a short slide and the jumps grow as you go (2 seconds, 5, 10... minutes, hours), with
  the start / end at the very edge of the screen. The video keeps playing until you let go.
- Double-tap the left or right side of a VOD to rewind or fast-forward, just like YouTube; keep
  tapping to go further.
- Muted parts of VODs are marked red on the seek bar, and a "Skip" button (or OK on a remote)
  jumps past the one you're in.
- Settings button right in the player, and playback picks up where it left off when you return.
- Battery percentage in fullscreen that fades out of the way; choose where it sits among the
  player buttons.
- Optional always-visible progress bar with times on VODs and clips, and chapter markers on
  the seek bar.
- A Chapters button on every VOD: jump straight to any chapter in fullscreen.
- Live streams keep playing when your earbuds disconnect or go in their case, and resuming a
  live stream always jumps back to live.
- Tap a paused mini player to resume it.

========================================
Browsing
- Favourite streamers: long-press a profile picture to add a gold ring and star. Favourites
  appear first in your Live list, with everyone else in alphabetical order, and can be grouped
  at the top of your Channels list too ("Favourites first" in its sort options).
- Long-press a channel in Following > Channels to jump straight to their chat, live or offline.
- Long-press a stream to open it audio-only.
- Clean stream cards: streamer, category and title, with text sizes you can adjust.
- Layouts that stay readable on big screens and TVs with lots of columns.
- Refresh everything with one button (or just tap the Live tab), and lists refresh themselves
  when you come back to the app. Every automatic refresh, reload and resume can be switched
  on or off in one place (Settings > UI > Automatic refresh).
- Optional fixed top bar that stays in place while you scroll.
- Back always takes you home to your Live list before leaving the app.
- Press Following again from its main page to jump straight to a freshly refreshed Live list.

========================================
Extras
- Chat commands: the ! button shows the channel's chat-bot commands and their replies (from
  Nightbot, StreamElements and Fossabot) in a big table you can scroll and search, with emotes shown as in chat, so you can
  see a streamer's !specs or !socials without asking in chat. Tap one to put it in your
  message box.
- Xtra5s Companion: a second, separate copy of the app that installs next to Xtra5s, with
  its own settings and login. Useful for running two streams as two apps (for example in
  split screen). Each copy updates itself.
- If the app crashes, the error is saved to a text file in your Download folder, and the next
  time the app opens it tells you, with a Share button to send the error.
